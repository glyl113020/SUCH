Ratings by group

All offices

Rating = average of S, U, C, H. All HO units are recorded as one HEAD OFFICE and all SO units as one SATELLITE OFFICE. Updates as soon as data is submitted or imported.

Visited · LOW Visited · NORMAL Visited · HIGH

Days show the actual visit dates. Visits planned for the month (no fixed day yet) are listed above the calendar; LOW branches can be visited any time in their half-year.

This quarter

Next quarter

NORMAL = Quarterly: one visit every quarter. LOW = Semi-Annual: one visit per half-year — H1 (Q1 or Q2) and H2 (Q3 or Q4); visited in Q1 means the next visit is any time in Q3 or Q4, and it is only late when the half-year ends. HIGH = Monthly. A branch turns Visited as soon as its visit is submitted or imported.

Schedule compliance

Scheduled = the branch is on the locked schedule for that quarter. The first visit of a branch's period counts as its scheduled visit; extra visits in the same period, or visits to branches not on the schedule, are "Visited – not scheduled". A locked schedule can't be changed; locking again only adds branches that became due.

Submit a data record

Add one device or user at a time. Pick the parameter, branch and serial (or employee); if it was recorded before, its last details are filled in — change only what is different and submit. The Date of inclusion is the date this record counts for (ratings are filtered by month and year). The score is computed by the system.
